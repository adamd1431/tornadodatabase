What do these bars mean?

Lower shear, warmer water and more mid-level moisture generally support tropical cyclone development. These are separate diagnostics, not an intensification probability or a combined score. Bar display scales: shear 60 → 0 kt, SST 20 → 32°C, humidity 0 → 100%.

Shear arrows point toward the 200 minus 850 hPa vector. GFS fields retain their own valid time; SST is a daily NOAA CRW analysis or labeled JPL MUR fallback. Core shear, SST anomalies and ventilation index are not supplied by these feeds.
