Tornado estimate formula

Experimental · For fun only. Please don’t take these estimates seriously or treat them as official tornado ratings.

Start with the strongest damage indicator. Add supporting evidence through a smooth curve that approaches 260 mph. Stronger evidence always maintains or raises the estimate; confidence adjusts the added support.

B = strongest selected indicator
S = 0.04 × sum of max(indicator − 100, 0), excluding one leading indicator
P = (S + X-factor points + Calculation points) × Confidence
Final = B + (260 − B) × (1 − exp(−P / 85))

Confidence and range calculation

A score of 5 gives 1.000×. Scores interpolate linearly toward these minimum / maximum multipliers: documentation 0.940 / 1.032; radar 0.980 / 1.026; environment 0.975 / 1.030; certainty 0.910 / 1.018.

Range half-width = 4.5 + 0.75 × (20 − documentation score − certainty score), in mph. Apply it to the final estimate, keeping the range within 40–260 mph.
