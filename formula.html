Contextual estimate model

Tornado estimate formula

This model starts with your base structural/DI estimate, then adds X-factor contextuals for evidence the structure may not capture. That keeps construction inside the DI estimate while still allowing rural violent tornadoes to score high when the contextual evidence is strong.

Final = clamp(40, 245, (Base Structural/DI + X-Factor Contextuals) x Confidence)
Base Structural/DI = direct estimate from damage indicators or calculation, 40-220 mph
X-Factor = Sparse Target + Ground + Vehicle + Vegetation + Debris + Radar + Path + Calculation Support
Confidence = Documentation x Uncertainty
