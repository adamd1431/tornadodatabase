Tornado estimate formula

This model starts with a structural wind estimate, then applies decimal contextual multipliers for ground marks, vehicle movement, vegetation damage, debris transport, radar, path behavior, documentation, and uncertainty. The goal is to reduce bias by making every boost tied to a visible evidence category.

Final = clamp(40, 250, Structural × Context × Documentation × Uncertainty)
Structural = Rating Anchor + DI Offset + Construction Offset
Context = 1 + Ground + Vehicle + Vegetation + Debris + Radar + Path Behavior
