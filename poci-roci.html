How the extraction works

The extractor locates a distinct grid pressure minimum within 300 km of the cyclone’s track position. It traces closed isobars every 2 mb and selects the highest isolated one before the next contour opens or joins another pressure system with at least 2 mb of basin depth. When a contour appears open, it checks the full global longitude domain to distinguish an open isobar from a clipped regional loop. ROCI is the average distance from the grid minimum to that contour over 360 bearings.

These are estimates from a 1° ensemble-mean reanalysis, not operational JTWC values. Small or weak cyclones may be unresolved. The central pressure printed on the map is the grid minimum; it does not replace the storm’s observed pressure. No value is returned when the outer contour cannot be established.

Results can differ from readings of a map drawn every 10 mb. Reported IBTrACS POCI and ROCI remain separate. The CKZ link uses the selected storm’s IBTrACS inputs with the explicitly chosen reanalysis POCI and ROCI. Environmental pressure is set directly to POCI (EP = POCI), without adding 2 mb.
