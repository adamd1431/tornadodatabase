Fujita cycloidal estimate

Cycloidal wind estimator

Enter the measured loop width, the radius from the tornado center to the suction-vortex path, and the parent tornado forward speed. The tool solves Fujita's cycloidal-mark equation for n, then estimates rotational and maximum ground-relative wind.

W / (2R) = [sqrt(n^2 - 1) - pi/2 + asin(1/n)] / n

Use the same distance unit for W and R. This is an experimental estimate from ground marks, not an official rating method.
