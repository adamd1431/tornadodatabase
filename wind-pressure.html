About the methods

CKZ (2009) uses the published low-latitude equation below 18° and the standard equation at 18° and above. Storm motion is editable. ADT / McIDAS-V reproduces SSEC’s documented CKZ conversion, including its 11-knot motion default and weak-system pressure adjustment.

All wind results are 1-minute sustained estimates. The CI option starts from an existing intensity number; satellite imagery is needed to derive CI itself. Pressure → wind numerically inverts the selected equation. More than one solution can exist; matches in the 25–250 knot calculation range are shown, with a minimum of 34 knots when a gale radius is present. These are empirical estimates; real storms can depart from the relationship.

Sources: Courtney & Knaff (2009)·SSEC / McIDAS-V equations & CI table·CIMSS ADT
