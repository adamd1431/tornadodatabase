Finding the right scan

Storm positions come from NOAA IBTrACS v04r01, using USA positions when available and explicitly labelled merged positions otherwise. Between records, positions are interpolated over gaps of six hours or less. Times outside the track need manual coordinates.

The automatic satellite is an operational-era suggestion. The page checks the public SSEC inventory and selects the nearest listed scan within three hours whose band and coverage include the storm. It shows the selected time separately from your requested time, and centers the plot on the requested-time track position. If the suggested satellite has no scan, automatic selection checks other archived satellites covering the storm. Satellite and band availability changes with the date. Use the SSEC inventory to check the archive, outages and exact scans.

McFETCH accounts have daily limits and may restrict recent dates. Registration requests are reviewed by SSEC. All scans render calibrated temperatures on a geographic map and export lossless PNG.

Built using the McFETCH API, inspired by GoldStandardBot. Pasted commands fill the storm, time and BD/grayscale enhancement. Plain !mcfetch and !mcfetch_nc both produce the same temperature-plot format. Plot timestamps come from the downloaded data, not the requested time.
