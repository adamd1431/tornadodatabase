Finding the right scan

Storm positions come from NOAA IBTrACS v04r01, using USA positions when available and explicitly labelled merged positions otherwise. Between records, positions are interpolated over gaps of six hours or less. Times outside the track need manual coordinates.

The automatic satellite is an operational-era suggestion. Satellite and band availability changes with the date. Use the SSEC inventory to check the archive, outages and exact scans. Visible imagery needs daylight.

McFETCH accounts have daily limits and may restrict recent dates. Registration requests are reviewed by SSEC. Images are requested as PNG; this page does not resize or recompress the returned file.
