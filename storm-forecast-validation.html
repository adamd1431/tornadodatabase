WHAT CHANGED

Fit the guidance, keep the test separate.

The old forecast averaged wind, pressure and position changes from six model families. V2 adds HWRF, HMON, COAMPS-TC, early guidance and environmental intensity guidance such as LGEM, Decay-SHIPS and CHIPS.

Nonnegative, regularized weights and bounded bias corrections vary by lead and region. Initial offsets decay over 48 hours in the selected corrections; a past intensity tendency is used where validation selected it. Missing members return their weight to the original baseline.

A field was promoted only after at least a 2% validation improvement. The Atlantic/eastern Pacific track failed that gate and retains the original method. The global track improvement has a confidence interval spanning no improvement.

NO FUTURE INITIALIZATION

Operational input. Final verification.

Forecasts start from archived operational CARQ analyses, not the final best track. Raw model cycles require a six-hour availability delay; early guidance requires three hours. Nominal issue time is analysis +3 hours.

Verification uses final NOAA IBTrACS USA agency fields at six-hour synoptic fixes. Provisional records are excluded. Final 2025 JTWC data were unavailable, so a separate set of whole 2024 storms provides the global test.

Actual publication times and archive revisions cannot be reconstructed exactly. This is a retrospective archive test, not proof of future operational skill. Best-track pressure can itself be an estimate.

Scope and reproducibility

The study collected 205 storm IDs across six ATCF basins. After eligibility and coverage checks, 197 distinct storms contributed 13,217 forecast–lead pairs. Whole storm IDs (IBTrACS SID) stay in one split. The 2024 global holdout was assigned by a fixed SID hash before errors were computed, with at least one storm per available basin. The selected model was refit on training + validation only, then frozen before the two test sets were scored.

The uncertainty ranges above are 95% paired bootstrap intervals, resampling whole storms 1,000 times. They describe uncertainty within this sample; they do not include all best-track or model-version uncertainty. Six-hour display steps interpolate coefficients between the evaluated 12, 24, 48, 72, 96 and 120-hour leads. Model disagreement ranges are not calibrated probabilities. Displayed GFS shear, humidity and ocean fields remain separate diagnostics.
